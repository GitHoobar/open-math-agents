# Selection and literature check

27 November 2026. I looked for a structural yes-or-no question and screened out problems with reported solutions or direct partial results. For example, the Owings sumset problem now has an affirmative-answer preprint, Green’s Problem 57 has a formal counterexample, and Green’s partition-into-sumsets question has published special-case progress. [Owings preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17333) · [Green 57 formal proof](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphaproof-nexus-results/blob/main/APNOutputs/AICollaborator/AdditiveCombinatorics/57.lean) · [Green 53 partial results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07918)

For Hofstadter’s set, the distinct-factor convention comes from the Erdős problem statement and Korsky’s preprint. I checked the original problem archive, Ben Green’s Problem 63, the OEIS entry, Korsky’s theorem, and the Formal Conjectures statement. The last source explicitly distinguishes its solved positive-lower-density theorem from its open natural-density question. The two questions concern the same set. [Erdős archive](https://www.erdosproblems.com/424) · [Green’s collection](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/greenbj/papers/open-problems.pdf) · [OEIS A005244](https://oeis.org/A005244) · [Korsky](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07910) · [Formal Conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/9097e2f90e66d2f6244209cb1c07f7120d17ec53/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/424.lean)

Indexed searches combined “Hofstadter”, “ab−1”, “A005244”, “Erdős 424” and “Korsky” with “natural density”, “convergence”, “asymptotic density”, “residue classes”, “every modulus” and “profinite”. I found the lower bound, the elementary modulo-three upper bound, and the separate open convergence statement. I found no proof that the counting proportion converges, no proof of oscillation, and no convergence estimate narrowing the gap between its liminf and limsup to zero. The lower-density theorem is substantial prior work. The sources do not establish that nobody has worked privately on convergence, or that no intermediary observation exists.

I developed the modular-projection proof to test the possibility of matching the lower and upper densities by periodic bounds. The positive-lower-density conclusion in each class uses Korsky’s theorem. I did not find a prior statement of the full modular lemma in the sources searched and do not claim first discovery of it. The natural-density question remains open.
