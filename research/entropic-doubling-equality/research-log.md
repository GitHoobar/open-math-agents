# Research record, 27 November 2026

The target is the converse equality characterization asked for immediately after Theorem 5 in §3.2 of arXiv:2403.07209v2. The authors give the Gaussian sufficient condition and an infinite-entropy obstruction to extending the converse without hypotheses. They explicitly leave the finite-entropy converse open. The note treats every dimension and includes the singular-noise boundary. The authors' article appeared in IEEE Transactions on Information Theory. I checked question wording and equation numbering against the author-linked revised arXiv manuscript. [Author publication list](https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~lg560/Publications.html) · [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2024.3460472)

I checked indexed searches for the paper title with “equality”, “large doubling”, “correction”, “Gaussian codebooks”, “finite entropy” and “Theorem 5”. OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar each returned the same five citing records, covering additive energy, de Finetti bounds, and equality or stability in Shannon's and Tao's inequalities. I inspected the full text of the closest later equality paper. Its discussion of doubling concerns the ordinary entropy jump, and its reference to the 2024 paper does not state the converse to equation (10). Crossref returned no updates linked to the original journal DOI. I found no explicit earlier answer or partial converse for the stated finite-entropy question. This search cannot establish that nobody worked on it. [OpenAlex citation list](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=cites:W4402774096) · [Later equality paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14021) · [Related additive-energy paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20813)

Gavalakis's earlier thesis also contains an antecedent, Propositions 5.3–5.4. Remark 5.3 identifies when the displayed correction term vanishes and observes that Gaussian inputs attain equality. Vanishing of that correction is different from equality in the inequality between its two sides. The subsequent paper expressly asks about the converse under finite entropy. [Thesis, §5.3](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/09f0644e-6303-4724-a8d8-92b88f560456/content)

The proof uses two classical results. The original authors already give the covariance-matched Gaussian entropy comparison in §1.1. Combining this comparison with Shannon's full two-summand inequality yields a chain with equality at both steps. The contribution claimed here is the explicit answer to the published question. An independent derivation in the note avoids using the equality theorem for Shannon’s inequality. In checking the endpoint assumptions, allowing zero noise produced a counterexample to the unrestricted wording. The note therefore treats deterministic and nonzero singular noise separately. For entropy of minus infinity the paper already gives a counterexample and we make no new claim.

The proof was checked by symbolic derivation in this session. There has been no external referee report or end-to-end proof-assistant verification.
